Privacy Policy — Clutch Shots Arcade Effective date: May 16, 2026 · Last updated: May 16, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how LiveStack Studios ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Clutch Shots Arcade (the "App") on iOS and iPadOS. We designed Clutch Shots Arcade to collect as little personal information as possible. 1. Information we do not collect We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not operate our own analytics servers or accounts system. We do not sell or rent any data to third parties. 2. Information collected through Apple services The App uses standard Apple frameworks. When you use these features, the relevant data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy: Game Center — If you sign in to Game Center, your Game Center alias and scores/achievements you earn are submitted to Apple's leaderboards and achievement system. We do not receive your Apple ID or real name. 3. Advertising (Google AdMob) The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as: Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission. Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP address. Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completions). If you decline tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads and will not use the IDFA. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. For details, see Google's Advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy. 4. Children's privacy Clutch Shots Arcade is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to comply with applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA and GDPR-K) by serving only non-personalized, child-appropriate ads where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it. 5. Data we store on your device The App stores the following locally on your device only: Game progress and high scores. Sound and settings preferences. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App removes it. 6. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you. Because we do not maintain user accounts, the most effective way to exercise these rights is to: Manage Game Center data through your Apple ID settings. Manage advertising preferences through iOS Tracking settings. Delete the App to remove all locally stored game data. You can also contact us at the address below. 7. Changes to this policy If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, post a notice in the App. 8.
